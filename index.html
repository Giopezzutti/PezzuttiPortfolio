About Me

Hi, I’m Giovanni. I work in data and analytics, with experience building reporting, forecasting, and operational solutions that help businesses run more effectively. My background in industrial engineering gave me a strong foundation in problem-solving and process thinking, and I enjoy applying that mindset to projects where data can improve clarity, efficiency, and decision-making. This portfolio is a space to share some of the work and ideas that reflect that approach.

I’ve always been drawn to work that combines analytical thinking with practical execution. I enjoy digging into the details, understanding how things work, and building solutions that can make a real difference. I’m also committed to continuous learning, whether through certifications, personal projects, or simply taking the time to explore new ideas and tools.

I’m especially drawn to projects where I can build something useful from the ground up. That could be a report, a process, a model, or a workflow, but what matters to me is creating something that brings more clarity and makes the work better for the people using it. I find that part of the process just as rewarding as the analysis itself.

Outside of work, bouldering has become one of my favorite ways to challenge myself. I enjoy the balance of problem-solving, movement, and persistence that it requires. For me, it is another way of practicing the same mindset I bring into my work: stay curious, keep improving, and enjoy the process

Portfolio

Forecasting For Retail Operations

This project is a full demand-forecasting workflow built around a label-ordering problem for a supermarket distribution center. The forecast is used as a planning signal that supports downstream operational decisions such as blank label purchasing, label printing, and pre-applying labels to selected containers.

Multi-Vendor ETL Pipeline

A Python ETL pipeline for ingesting daily CSV exports from third-party delivery platforms, validating vendor-specific schemas, transforming each file into a normalized "silver" dataset, publishing outputs to a shared folder structure, and sending run summaries by email. This project automates a recurring reporting workflow across multiple vendors with different raw formats while preserving a single downstream output shape for analytics and operations.

Inventory Management for Coffee Shop

This analytical report outlines the process of developing a procurement system and inventory management strategy for a fast growing chain of coffee shops in New York City. The analysis is based on historical transactions from one of the stores, utilizing Python as the primary tool for data management, cleaning, analysis, and export. Leveraging a programming language like Python offers the benefits of reproducibility and automation, facilitating future analyses across multiple stores.
